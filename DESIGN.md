## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the tension between traditional cinematic prestige and generative AI synthesis. Designed for AI Film Producer Bidyawant Pradhan, it pairs the spatial purity of an haute-couture gallery with the rigorous precision of an avant-garde production atelier. 

The aesthetic is ultra-minimalist, architectural, and restrained. By utilizing vast fields of pure white and subtle bone gray, visual priority is reserved for cinematic compositions, stills, and high-fidelity video reels. The emotional resonance is one of quiet authority, technical sophistication, and uncompromising discernment. Interface elements retreat until summoned, operating through micro-hairline structures and calculated editorial typography.

## Colors

The palette establishes an ultra-high-contrast gallery environment anchored by light tones and carbon typography:

- **Canvas & Backdrops:** Base ground is pure white (`#FFFFFF`), stepped subtly down to Bone Light (`#F8F9FA`) for surface structural tiering and Pale Mist (`#F1F3F5`) for recessed canvases or video wells.
- **Ink & Typography:** Primary contrast is achieved through Obsidian Ink (`#0B0B0C`) for display headlines and key focal points, Off-Black (`#18181B`) for readable body passages, and Muted Carbon (`#52525B`) for metadata, technical classifications, and timecodes.
- **Accents:** Warm Champagne Gold (`#B58D29` / `#9A7B2C`) is utilized sparingly—reserved exclusively for active states, AI model generation markers, curated laurels, and deliberate focal calls to action.
- **Structural Hairlines:** Boundaries, framing corners, and spatial divisions rely entirely on crisp 1px borders in Zinc Hairline (`#E4E4E7`).

## Typography

The typographic architecture establishes a three-way dynamic:

1. **Syne (Display & Headlines):** Sculptural, expressive, and distinctively editorial. Used for production titles, cinematic headings, and dramatic statements. Rendered tight and dense with reduced letter-spacing.
2. **Hanken Grotesk (Body & Prose):** Highly disciplined, modern, and invisible in operation. Balances the expressive headlines with neutral, highly legible reading lines for synopses, essays, and production notes.
3. **Space Mono (Labels & Technical Annotations):** Precision mono-width typography handles AI model parameters, aspect ratios, frame rates, scene timestamps, and prompt architecture credits. Always displayed in deliberate uppercase with generous letter-spacing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column architectural grid inspired by broadsheet editorial spreads and screening room storyboards. Whitespace is structural rather than decorative—framing media and text like mounted gallery prints.

- **Grid Rhythms:** A 12-column layout on desktop (`1280px+`) with `2.5rem` gutters and `4rem` outer borders. Drops to 6 columns on tablet (`768px - 1024px`) with `1.5rem` gutters, and 4 columns on mobile (`< 768px`) with `1rem` gutters and `1.5rem` outer margins.
- **Screening Rhythms:** Media containers break strict vertical bounds using dynamic 16:9, 2.39:1 (anamorphic), or 4:5 aspect ratios, anchored by hairline perimeter guidelines.
- **Section Pacing:** Large breathing spaces (`space-xl`) separate distinct production case studies to avoid visual contamination between visual worlds.

## Elevation & Depth

This system intentionally rejects skeuomorphic blurs, drop shadows, and heavy volumetric depth. Elevation is communicated entirely through tonal layering, crisp borders, and optical stacking:

- **Level 0 (Gallery Base):** `#FFFFFF` as the infinite canvas.
- **Level 1 (Card & Modular Ground):** `#F8F9FA` bounded by a strict `1px solid #E4E4E7` border.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover/Floating Overlays):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces with a 1px border shift to `#0B0B0C` or the warm champagne accent `#B58D29`. 
- **Modal & Cinema Lightbox:** Pure black `#0B0B0C` backdrop at 90% opacity, cutting out ambient light to transition the user from gallery viewing to direct cinematic screening.
- **No Diffused Shadow:** Cast shadows are omitted in favor of flat architectural precision and razor-thin hairlines.

## Shapes

The design system is strictly sharp (`roundedness: 0`). Corner radiuses across all standard components—buttons, media tiles, cards, tags, and inputs—are fixed at `0px`.

This non-rounded, razor-sharp discipline references physical gallery plinths, photographic contact sheets, 35mm film slides, and modernist editorial journals. Precision comes from perpendicular lines and hairline intersections rather than smoothed corners.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#0B0B0C` fill, `#FFFFFF` text, `0px` radius, `Space Mono` typography in uppercase (`label-md`). Hover transitions smoothly to `#B58D29` with black text.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Transparent fill, `1px solid #0B0B0C`, `#0B0B0C` text. Hover reverses fill to `#0B0B0C` and text to `#FFFFFF`.
- **Text Link:** Underlined with a `1px` offset border that shifts color to `#B58D29` on hover.

### Chips & Metadata Badges
- **Technical Tag:** `1px solid #E4E4E7` background `#F8F9FA`, `Space Mono` (`label-sm`), color `#52525B`. Used for specs like `[MIDJOURNEY V6]`, `[RUNWAY GEN-3]`, `[2.39:1 ANAMORPHIC]`.
- **Status / Laurel Tag:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#B58D29`, border `1px solid #B58D29`.

### Media Cards & Film Modules
- Sharp rectangular frame with `1px solid #E4E4E7`.
- Hover introduces an interior 1px frame highlight in `#0B0B0C` or `#B58D29` and reveals prompt metadata and generation telemetry in an overlaid light bone drawer (`#F8F9FA`).
- Images and video previews maintain a strict grayscale-to-full-color transition or play on viewport entry.

### Form Inputs & Prompts
- Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F8F9FA` with a bottom-only or full `1px solid #E4E4E7` border.
- Focus state hard-shifts border to `1px solid #0B0B0C`. 
- Placeholder text in `#52525B` using `Hanken Grotesk`.

### Lists & Filmographies
- Tabular architectural format. Rows separated by `1px solid #E4E4E7` dividers.
- Column structure: Year (`Space Mono`), Project Title (`Syne`), Role/Model Pipeline (`Hanken Grotesk`), Action/Link (`Space Mono`). Hovering turns the entire row subtle bone gray (`#F1F3F5`).

### Lightbox & Media Player
- Stripped-down playback controls using geometric vector glyphs.
- Progress scrub bar is a single `1px` baseline (`#E4E4E7`) with the active playhead tracking in `#B58D29`.
